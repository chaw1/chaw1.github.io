问题The problem

万级任务的批量提交耗时超过 2 分钟，影响平台操作体验。Submitting a batch of 10k tasks took over two minutes, slowing down the platform workflow.

我的工作My contribution

作为研发负责人，主导平台架构与批量任务性能优化，并把容错验证纳入验收。As Head of Engineering, led platform architecture and batch-task performance work, including fault-tolerance validation.

结果与验证Outcome & validation

提交耗时降至 0.89 秒；故障注入验证覆盖 280 次重试场景。该指标衡量提交阶段，不代表所有任务的处理完成时间。Submission time fell to 0.89 seconds, with fault-injection validation covering 280 retry scenarios. This measures submission, not completion of all tasks.
